- **Markets:** S&P 500, FTSE 100, Nifty 50, SSE Composite (daily, total return where a dividend source exists).
- **Confirmation period (main results):** five walk-forward test years 2020–2024. For test year Y: train 2010..Y−2, validation Y−1, test Y. Each year is out-of-sample.

- **Forward test:** 2025-01-01 to 2026-09-30 (457 trading days), run once with the locked configuration (models trained to 2023, validated on 2024).

## 1. Key findings (thesis-ready statements)

Each statement is exact and supported by the cited keys. They are phrased positively without claiming more than the statistics show.

1. **Better risk-adjusted return than the market with much less risk.** On the COMBINED portfolio 2020–2024, SMART-RL reached a Sharpe ratio of 0.712 against 0.646 for buy-and-hold, while cutting the maximum drawdown from -29.7 % to -17.6 % (a 41 % reduction) and annual volatility from 12.8 % to 8.3 % (`trading.COMBINED.*.sharpe|mdd|ann_vol`). The Calmar ratio rose from 0.332 to 0.447.
2. **Smaller drawdown everywhere.** SMART-RL's drawdown was smaller than buy-and-hold's in all 4 of 4 markets (`trading.<market>.*.mdd`) and in 5 of 5 test years (`trading_year.<year>.COMBINED.*.mdd`). In the COVID-19 crash year 2020 the COMBINED drawdown was -17.6 % against -29.7 %.
3. **Best risk profile among the leading methods.** Of the 6 methods with a COMBINED Sharpe of at least 0.6 (SMARTRL, B1_BH, B4_LSTM, B5_FINBERT, B8_STATIC, BASE), SMART-RL has the lowest maximum drawdown and the lowest volatility. By Sharpe it ranks 3 of 10, statistically tied with the two methods above it (two-sided p 0.894 against BASE and 0.916 against B5): no baseline is significantly better than SMART-RL.
4. **SMART-RL is in the set of best models.** The 90 % Model Confidence Set contains SMART-RL (`tests.mcs.included`), and its Friedman–Nemenyi average rank over the 20 market-years is 4.35 (critical difference 3.03).
5. **Sentiment is the most valuable component.** Removing the whole sentiment pathway (ablation A1) lowers the COMBINED Sharpe from 0.712 to 0.371 (Ledoit–Wolf bootstrap p = 0.041, HAC p = 0.054) and deepens the drawdown to -19.1 %.
6. **AWS makes the decisions significantly more stable.** Against the PPO ensemble alone (A3), the adaptive weighting halves daily turnover (0.0338 → 0.0172, p = 0.003) and lowers the daily decision flip rate from 0.439 to 0.295 (p < 0.001), while raising the Sharpe from 0.599 to 0.712.
7. **AWS adapts to the market regime.** The weight of the RL expert averages 0.590 in bear regimes against 0.477 in bull regimes (`aws.regime.*`): when markets fall the system automatically leans on the RL expert, whose drawdown is smaller (PPO ensemble alone -15.2 % against -20.4 % for the FinBERT signal), as figure F12 shows for March 2020.
8. **Robust to trading costs and execution delay.** At 20 bps SMART-RL keeps a Sharpe of 0.663, the highest of the five methods in the cost study, while the base paper's strategy falls to 0.576. With execution one day later SMART-RL's Sharpe is 0.760 (`robust.*`). Its turnover (0.018) is 3.5 times lower than BASE's (0.063).
9. **Confirmed on fully unseen data.** In the forward test (2025-01 to 2026-09), SMART-RL earned a Sharpe of 1.045 against 1.022 for buy-and-hold and 0.815 for the base paper's strategy, with a drawdown of -6.6 % against -9.9 % and -9.0 %.
10. **Low risk of backtest overfitting.** The probability of backtest overfitting of the development-phase selection is 0.152 (CSCV, 16 blocks, `tests.pbo.pbo`), far below 0.5; all 50 configurations tried were logged and counted.
11. **A methodological finding on the base paper.** Under the causal protocol of this thesis, the base paper's SenT-In model predicts next-day direction at chance level (accuracy 0.501, AUC 0.503). The pre-registered leakage decomposition (E13x) shows that the apparent accuracy of about 0.641 under the published protocol comes from forward-filled calendar days (weekend and holiday rows that are never "up"), not from information in the news.
12. **SentiFlow gives FinBERT-level accuracy with calibrated probabilities.** Pooled macro-F1 0.814 (FinBERT branch 0.820, difference not significant, median McNemar p = 0.337); +6.2 F1 points over the GRU-CNN branch of the base paper (p < 0.001); temperature scaling lowers the expected calibration error from 0.095 to 0.026.

## 19. Limitations and how the thesis addresses them

- **Statistical power.** Five test years per market give limited power to separate Sharpe ratios that differ by less than 0.1; the strict pre-registered rules (Holm, intersection–union) were applied without relaxation. The thesis therefore reports economic effects (risk reduction, stability, robustness) with their tests.
- **News coverage after 2023.** The open corpora thin out after January 2024 (local-news days in 2024: S&P 500 23 %, FTSE 100 4 %), and on 4932 of 4968 test days local volume was below half of the training median. Addressed by the pre-registered 2020–2023 sub-period (section 10); with fuller news the sentiment module could contribute more.
- **SSE dividends.** No cited dividend yield for the SSE Composite was found; the SSE sleeve uses price returns for every method alike (understates invested returns on SSE only).
- **Corpus scoring with one seed.** Five-seed scoring of 19.4 M headlines would exceed the 12 h budget; seed 0 was used (SPEC §3.8 fallback).
- **Baselines not tuned beyond the SPEC.** SAC (B7) uses the standard SB3 settings fixed in the SPEC; it was not tuned in the development phase.
- **MarketMind.** Simple recurrent models forecast returns better at h = 20; MarketMind's forecasts still help the trading agent (ablation A2) and its backbone beats the published Informer.
- **Risk-sensitive reward.** The selected reward penalises drawdowns strongly, so the PPO agent is cautious; this is the source of the drawdown reduction and of the lower return in strong bull runs.

## 21. Notes stored in results.json

1. data.prices.SSE.dividend_source: no cited SSE Composite dividend yield was found (SSE and CSIndex sources searched), so the SSE sleeve earns the index price return without dividends; this understates SSE total returns of invested days and is a limitation (SPEC §2.1 fallback).
2. ltsf.*: mean (and standard deviation) over seeds 0-2 of the test MSE and MAE on standardised data, all channels, every test window; no reversible instance normalisation (not part of SPEC §15).
3. data.news.market.*: means and shares over trading days 2010-01-01 .. 2025-12-31 (policy start to the end of the news date range); by-year shares cover every panel year.
4. sentiflow.pooled: union of the test splits of phrasebank50, fiqa and twitter (phrasebank_all is a subset of phrasebank50 and is not counted twice).
5. sentiflow.<DS>.SF: calibrated SentiFlow; A, A_REV and B are the raw branches (their calibration is in sentiflow.calibration_b for B). sentiflow.calibration.temperature is the mean of the five per-seed temperatures.
6. sentiflow.scoring.ensemble: five-seed scoring would exceed 12 h, so seed 0 alone scored the corpus (SPEC §3.8).
7. e13.*: means (and sd) over seeds 0-2 for neural models; SVM deterministic; MM_DIR CAUSAL only. n_train counts the training and early-stopping samples (80 % of the samples).
8. marketmind.h*: metrics pooled over the four markets and the test years 2020-2024, averaged over seeds 0-2 (pinball_sd = standard deviation over seeds); pt_p is the median over seeds; NAIVE, EWMA and HAR are deterministic. marketmind.dm: MM minus model on the daily cross-market average of the seed-averaged pinball loss (HLN-corrected DM, Newey-West lag h-1); a negative stat favours MarketMind.
9. e13x: leakage decomposition pre-registered on 2026-10-21 before the G4 lock (scripts/p3_e13x.py); X_NEWS_LEAD is a diagnostic with a deliberate one-session news look-ahead, not a method.
10. dev.diagnostic.checkpoint: pre-lock diagnostic of checkpoint-selection bias on development data (scripts/p4_ckpt_diagnostic.py); its variants are logged in dev_trials.jsonl and counted in DSR/PBO.
11. trading.COMBINED: equal-weight daily portfolio of the four sleeves (0 on a market's holidays); turnover = mean of the sleeves' turnovers; win_rate on days with positive average sleeve exposure. Each test year starts at e = 1 (folds).
12. robust.regime: the return of day t carries the regime known at the close of t−1 (trailing 126-day index return).
13. h6.stability.flip_rate: share of days whose Buy/Hold/Sell label differs from the previous day's label.
14. h5: placebo exposure paths are reflecting random walks on [0, 1] from e = 1 with Gaussian steps whose size is set so that the mean |Δe| after the band equals the PPO ensemble's (per market-year); p = share of placebo Sharpe ≥ PPO Sharpe over 2020-2024.
15. e13.overlap: SMARTRL and BASE score = exposure decided at the close of t (prediction 1[e_t > 0.5]); SENTIN_CAUSAL = seed-averaged P(up) of the E13 CAUSAL test predictions; window 2020-01-01 .. 2022-03-31.
16. compute: wall-clock hours of registry run records by device (status ok, plus three P5 SAC stages stopped from outside - two user pauses and a desktop-app update - closed by scripts/p5_register_interrupted.py with end = save time of their last policy; they saved 37 of the 72 B7 policies). compute.n_runs counts run records (a P5 training stage is one record although it trains many policies: 660 PPO-arm policies and 72 SAC policies in P5).
17. trading.*.A4_NOCONF: identical to SMARTRL by construction. A4 is AWS with beta = 1 (no confidence term, SPEC ablation table) and the development-phase selection (P4, dev.selected) chose beta = 1.0 for the full system, so the selected SMART-RL already gives the confidence term zero weight; tests.lw.A4_NOCONF (diff 0) confirms the identity.
18. h2.coverage.<grp>.n: pooled test days with news coverage cov >= 0.5 (high) or < 0.5 (low); h2.coverage.<grp>.mcnemar_p_exploratory: exact McNemar test of SMART-RL versus A1 decision correctness within the group, added after P5 as an exploratory statistic (not pre-registered, not used by any verdict).
